# Refund Policy

This refund policy applies to commercial access, premium plans, support bundles, and any packaged version of the repository sold to end users or businesses.

## 1. Eligibility

Refunds may be considered under the following conditions:

- the product was not delivered or access was not granted
- the purchased package does not match the description in the commercial offer
- the buyer requests a refund within the stated refund period

## 2. Non-refundable situations

Refunds are generally not granted when:

- the buyer has already received access and used the product extensively
- support or installation services have already been performed
- the buyer changed their mind after receiving access
- the buyer has downloaded or used the repository beyond the stated refund period

## 3. Refund period

The default refund window is 7 days from purchase unless otherwise stated on the checkout page.

## 4. How to request a refund

Users must contact the provider using the commercial support email and include:

- name and email
- order or transaction reference
- date of purchase
- reason for the refund request

## 5. Processing time

Refund requests are reviewed within a reasonable time, typically 3 to 10 business days, depending on the payment processor and the complexity of the request.

## 6. Chargebacks

If a user opens a chargeback without first attempting to resolve the issue through the support process, the provider may suspend access and deny future support until the matter is resolved.

## 7. Updates

This policy may be updated at any time and will apply as published in the latest version.
