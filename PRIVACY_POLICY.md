# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how contact information, support data, and transaction references are handled when a commercial package is sold.

## 1. Information we may collect

We may collect:

- name and contact details
- email address
- payment reference information
- purchase history
- technical support messages
- installation or configuration details necessary to provide support

## 2. Purpose of collection

Information is used to:

- process purchases
- provide access and support
- improve the product and documentation
- communicate updates, bugs, and support information

## 3. Payment providers

If you use a payment provider such as Mercado Pago, the payment processor may collect additional information under its own privacy policy. This project does not store or manage your payment credentials directly unless you explicitly configure a custom checkout flow.

## 4. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for support, compliance, or transaction records.

## 5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share information only with trusted service providers required to deliver support or payment processing, and only under confidentiality obligations.

## 6. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect user information. However, no system is completely secure, so users acknowledge that data transmission over the internet may carry some risk.

## 7. Your rights

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information where applicable by local law.

## 8. Cookies and tracking

If a landing page or storefront uses analytics or tracking tools, their use will be stated clearly on the site.

## 9. Third-party links

The project may link to external services or documentation. Those sites have their own privacy practices and are not controlled by this project.
