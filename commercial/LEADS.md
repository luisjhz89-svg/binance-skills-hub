# Lead capture workflow

This file describes a simple way to generate leads without a heavy backend.

## Goal

Collect customer interest, qualify the lead, and then contact them personally via email.

## Workflow

1. The customer fills the form in the landing page.
2. The form opens a `mailto:` client or sends the information to a configured inbox.
3. The support team replies within 24 hours.
4. The customer is assigned to the best plan or receives a personalized proposal.

## Suggested conversion flow

- Free plan: invite the customer to try Lite.
- Starter: good for a single skill or early user.
- Pro: best for developers and power users.
- Pro Anual: strongest upsell for recurring value.
- Enterprise: custom project and support.

```
Hola [Nombre],

```
